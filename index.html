📚 Cybersecurity Education

🎣

Recognize Phishing

Check website domains carefully. Be suspicious of urgent messages asking for passwords or financial details.

🔐

Protect Your Wallet

Never share your wallet recovery phrase or private key. Use trusted wallet software and verify transaction details.

🚫

Avoid Investment Scams

Guaranteed profits, pressure to deposit money, and demands for extra withdrawal fees are important warning signs.

🔑

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra authentication step to important accounts and use unique, strong passwords.

📱

Secure Your Phone

Install updates, use a screen lock, and avoid installing apps from unknown sources.

🧾

Keep Evidence

For suspected fraud, preserve relevant messages, transaction references, dates, and screenshots without exposing secrets.
